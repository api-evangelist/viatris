Viatris Inc. is a global healthcare company formed in November 2020 through the combination of Mylan and Pfizer's Upjohn division. Viatris is one of the world's largest producers of Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients (APIs) and generic medicines, serving patients in more than 165 countries.

## Overview

Viatris focuses on branded generics, complex generics, biosimilars, and branded specialty medicines. The company has undergone significant strategic restructuring, including divesting its India API (Active Pharmaceutical Ingredient) business and women's healthcare business. No public developer technology API program or developer portal has been identified; the company operates in B2B pharmaceutical supply chain contexts without a public technology integration API offering.

**Note:** In the pharmaceutical industry, "API" refers to Active Pharmaceutical Ingredient — the biologically active component of a drug product — not a web/software API. Viatris is a major API manufacturer in this pharmaceutical sense.
